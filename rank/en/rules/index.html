Bidding & ranking rules

The rules live here, and apply to everyone equally.

1. Highest bid wins

The board is sorted by current bid, highest first. To take a higher rank, bid above the slot's current amount; a raise takes effect immediately.

2. Ties go to the first bid

When amounts match, the earlier submission ranks first. Timestamps are public and can be checked line by line.

3. Minimum raise

Each raise is at least 1 unit. The demo offers quick steps of 1 / 10 / 100 / 500.

4. Slot duration

Paid slots run for a display period, then reopen for bidding. Historical sales and removals are kept permanently and can be traced any time.

5. Transparent settlement

The demo does not charge money. Production requires identity verification, escrow and a licensed payment channel, with the advertising nature clearly disclosed.
