Rank up in four steps

From picking a slot to getting seen, the flow is plain to see.

01

Pick a slot

Find the spot you want on the board and check its current bid.

02

Place a bid

Enter an amount above the current bid and choose a raise step.

03

Confirm the lead

Once active, the ranking refreshes instantly and you see your new position.

04

Get seen

During the display period your product holds that slot, with visibility data available throughout.

Why you can trust it

A paid board lives or dies by trust, so we make what we can public.

Every bid is logged

Each raise carries an amount and timestamp, so anyone can check how a rank was reached.

Paid slots are labelled

Ranked slots carry a clear Paid slot tag, distinct from editorial content.

One rule for all

Tie-breaking and minimum-raise rules are written on the page and never bend for anyone.

No charges in the demo

This is a product demo. Bidding costs nothing and asks for no payment details.
