About Us ZouIC

ZouIC is an open-source development board aggregation platform, offering one-stop hardware development resources to developers worldwide.

Our mission

Make hardware development simpler and innovation more accessible. We work to lower the barrier to embedded development, helping developers quickly find the right boards and project resources.

Our vision

To become a leading open-source hardware resource platform that connects developers, hardware vendors, and open-source communities, and to build a thriving hardware development ecosystem.

Our values

Open sharing, innovation-driven, user first, continuous improvement. We believe in the power of open source and are committed to democratizing hardware development.

Platform features

Extensive board database

Covers 20 mainstream development boards with detailed specs and side-by-side comparison

A wealth of demo projects

Aggregates 24 open source projects across sensors, IoT, AI and more

Smart search

Filter by chip, use case, or protocol to find the resources you need fast

Active community

Contributions from developers around the world, with open-source projects updated continuously
