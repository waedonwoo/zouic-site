Six things to check before backing open hardware on CrowdSupply

Crowdfunding is not in-stock, and open has a scope

GeekWaedon @GeekWaedon · November 2026

This is not the same as buying a development board

Most CrowdSupply projects are crowdfunding campaigns: you are backing a device that is not yet in mass production, and delivery time, final specification and after-sales support can all change. That is not a flaw — it is how this kind of platform works — but the rules are worth reading before you pay.

ZouIC currently indexes 15 CrowdSupply projects, each listed on the CrowdSupply source page.

Six checks before ordering

  1. Ask exactly what is open — the full trio: hardware design (schematic/PCB), firmware and documentation. Open firmware alone does not make it open hardware.
  2. Check the hardware licence variant: for CERN OHL, note whether it is S (strongly reciprocal), W (weakly reciprocal) or P (permissive) — that decides whether you may keep a modified design closed.
  3. Confirm the design files come in an editable native format, not just PDF or Gerber; this matches the principle in How to read an open-hardware licence.
  4. Check the delivery status: is the project page showing crowdfunding, in production or shipped? Shipped projects often have a visible history of later firmware updates.
  5. Look at firmware and community maintenance: the last commit date and whether issues get answers say more about the experience ahead than the campaign copy does.
  6. Contact the project team before reselling or building on it: trademarks and commercial licensing usually fall outside an open-source licence.

If you just want a board you can use this week

Crowdfunding suits people chasing what is new, not someone who needs to get started today. The open-source boards section lists mainstream boards available now, such as the ESP32-S3-DevKitC and the Raspberry Pi Pico, with more mature documentation and communities. Frequently asked questions

Is everything on CrowdSupply open source?

Most projects focus on open hardware, but the scope varies (hardware / firmware / documentation). Confirm it on each project page.

Are crowdfunding delivery dates fixed?

No. Both the delivery date and final specification can change. Check the current status on the project page before ordering.

Does this site sell anything on my behalf?

No. ZouIC only indexes and links; clicking through takes you to the original project page.
