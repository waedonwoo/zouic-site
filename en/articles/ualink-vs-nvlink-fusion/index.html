The open-interconnect spec is two years ahead of the silicon: UALink, ESUN and the NVLink timing gap

Compute industry notes · GeekWaedon @GeekWaedon

GeekWaedon @GeekWaedon · November 2026

The real state of the scale-up interconnect standards fight in 2026 is this: the open camp has written thorough specifications and is slow at shipping silicon. Leading on paper does not mean a customer can take delivery this year.

Three routes, three governance models

OptionGovernanceKey figuresAvailability in 2026
NVLink 5/6 + FusionNVIDIA proprietary; Fusion is licensableNVLink 6: 3.6 TB/s per GPU; NVL72 domain of 72 GPUs, 576 dies plannedShips with the Rubin platform; Fusion customers still buy NVSwitch
UALink 1.0 / 2.0Open consortium specification (1.0 in 2025-04, 2.0 in 2026-04)200G/lane, up to 1,024 accelerators in one domain; 2.0 adds in-network computing, a management plane and chiplets (UCIe 3.0)Native 1.0 silicon only reaches labs in H2 2026, with volume production around 2027
OCP ESUN / SUE-TOCP open forum; membership grew from 12 to 175+Ethernet L2/L3 frame extensions, a 4-byte slim header (AFH); LLR + CBFC borrowed from UECRuns on standard Ethernet switch silicon, so it lands fastest

There is also an easily missed fourth option: PCIe 6.0 (64 GT/s) / 7.0 (128 GT/s) as a scale-up physical layer. AWS Trainium3's NeuronLink takes this route — lower bandwidth density, but the ecosystem is already there. Signal Integrity Journal's comparison puts NVLink 6 at 3.6 TB/s against 100-200 GB/s per GPU for scale-out: a full order of magnitude, and the physical reason why scale-up cannot be improvised with ordinary Ethernet.

AMD's transitional design is the most revealing

The Helios rack uses 72 MI455X accelerators for about 260 TB/s of scale-up bandwidth, in the same aggregate class as NVIDIA's Vera Rubin NVL72. But it did not wait for native UALink switch silicon: the rack uses Broadcom's 102.4 Tbps Tomahawk 6 switch chip and runs UALink over Ethernet through a custom I/O die, putting the protocol on 800G Ethernet physics first. HPE has already shipped a commercial system using Juniper switches.

That is the concrete cost of leading on specifications while lagging in silicon: UALink consortium chair Kurtis Bowman himself says it will take until around April 2027 and version 3.0 to match NVIDIA on performance and release cadence. On native silicon, Astera Labs' 320-lane Scorpio X supports both NVLink Fusion and UALink, and Upscale AI's SkyHammer plans Q4 samples — samples, note, not production.

More subtle: NVIDIA sits on ESUN's founding member list while continuing to invest in NVLink. It is the same play as pushing InfiniBand while joining UEC: treat open standards not as a threat but as a hedge and a customer option. To win, the open camp first has to fill in the unglamorous parts — switch silicon, drivers, communication libraries — rather than adding more features to the specification.

One honest line for procurement teams

For rack contracts signed in 2026, the scale-up domain effectively offers two choices: NVIDIA's NVL72 / Rubin complete systems, or a transitional combination such as AMD Helios — open protocol plus merchant Ethernet switch silicon. A genuinely multi-vendor native UALink domain will not have acceptable, verifiable hardware before 2027. Writing 2027 specification capabilities into 2026 tender requirements is manufacturing your own delivery risk.

Bottom line

Standards fights are never settled by spec sheets; they are settled by who ships volume silicon first, who gets adopted by mainstream frameworks first, and who acquires a second supplier first. UALink 2.0 and ESUN 1.0 have established the open side's technical legitimacy. Over the next 12 months the things worth watching are samples and interoperability testing of native UALink switch silicon, communication efficiency in real Helios deployments, and whether UALink 3.0 actually arrives in spring 2027. Until then, NVLink's moat is not bandwidth — it is the timetable.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fundamental difference between UALink and NVLink?

NVLink is NVIDIA's proprietary protocol and switching system; Fusion only licenses access while NVSwitch still provides the switching layer. UALink is an open consortium specification that allows accelerators from different vendors in one domain, but there is no volume-produced native switch chip in 2026.

Why does ESUN matter?

ESUN runs scale-up semantics on standard Ethernet switch silicon using frame extensions and a slim header. With more than 175 members — NVIDIA included among the founders — it can land faster than waiting for dedicated UALink silicon.

Can I buy a multi-vendor scale-up rack today?

Essentially not in 2026. AMD Helios uses UALink over Ethernet as a transitional design, native UALink silicon is expected in volume only in 2027, and cross-vendor interoperability testing is still under way.
