Matchmaking service

What I did for you is to clarify the requirements and allocate resources accordingly

Finding materials/searching for materials

Shortage materials, discontinued materials, niche models, small batch samples - help you sort out part numbers and alternative paths, match available resources.

Domestic alternative selection

Without drawing conclusions for you, provide alternative ideas, parameter comparisons, and feasibility assessments, leaving the decision-making power to you.

Supply chain resource docking

Connect the demand side with the available supply side, and have both parties negotiate and transact directly. I withdraw from the transaction process.

Selection and feasibility assessment

Based on device cognition, make initial judgments: whether the part number is true, whether the parameters are correct, and whether this path is likely to be blocked.

Suitable for finding me

  • Solution provider/electronic engineer: prototype, small batch, unable to find materials or need to replace
  • Procurement: Out of stock, long lead time, need for a second source of goods
  • A team that wants to do domestic substitution and needs to conduct feasibility assessment first
  • People who are willing to clarify their requirements, part numbers, and budget ranges

Don't do these, let's clarify first

  • Not a trader: no stockpiling, no profit margin, no direct trading
  • No commitment to stock, no guarantee that it will definitely be found
  • Do not touch part numbers with unknown sources or questionable origins
  • Do not engage in speculation, price gouging, or hoarding
Collaboration Process

Four steps, clean and efficient

From requirements to integration, every step clearly states what to do and what not to do, without any detours

1

Requirement submission

Please specify the part number/model, quantity, delivery time, budget range, and purpose (prototype, small batch, mass production) in the email or form below.

2

Sorting and preliminary judgment

Free round of requirement review and feasibility assessment: whether the part number matches, roughly which path to take, and whether there are any obvious pitfalls.

3

Resource matching

Match available suppliers within accessible resources and bring both parties together; At this stage, an NDA can be signed to protect the requirement information.

4

Docking and Exiting

Direct negotiation and transaction between both parties; I will withdraw from the trading process without any commission, involvement in funds, or performance.
