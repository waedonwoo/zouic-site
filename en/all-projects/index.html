reTerminal

An HMI terminal from Seeed Studio. Based on the Raspberry Pi CM4 (quad-core Cortex-A72 @ 1.5 GHz), it includes a 5-inch IPS touchscreen (1280×720), 4 GB RAM + 32 GB eMMC, dual-band Wi-Fi/Bluetooth 5.0, Gigabit Ethernet and a crypto co-processor. Raspberry Pi OS comes preinstalled; suited to industrial HMI and IoT edge gateway scenarios.

Quantum Mini Linux Dev Kit

One of the world's smallest Linux development boards, launched by GeekWaedon with Seeed Studio. The SoM, Quark-N (Allwinner H3 quad-core Cortex-A7 + Mali400 GPU), measures just 31×22 mm; the Atom-N carrier board is 40×35 mm and integrates a microphone, MPU6050, IPS screen and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth. GPL-3.0 licensed.

Planck-Pi

An extremely compact, low-cost Linux development board based on the Allwinner F1C200s SoC. The PCB measures just 31×22 mm and integrates DDR + eMMC, with most GPIO exposed through an M.2 Key-A edge connector for easy stacking with carrier boards. GPL-3.0 licensed; core hardware of GeekWaedon's "Quantum Plan".
