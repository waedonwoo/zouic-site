Open-source licenses

ZouIC aggregates many open-source projects released under a wide range of licenses. Below are the most common open-source licenses on GitHub.

Important notice:The license texts below come from official sources and are unmodified. Before using any open-source project, read its license terms carefully to make sure your use complies with them.

Open hardware licenses

OPEN HARDWARE

Boards, schematics and hardware design files often use licenses that are separate from software. These are short summaries only; the official license text governs your rights and obligations.

CERN Open Hardware Licence v2

A copyleft family for hardware design with three variants: S (strong copyleft), W (weak copyleft) and P (permissive), covering modification, manufacture and distribution.

Read the official license

TAPR Open Hardware License

An early open-hardware license that requires design documentation to be published and derivative designs to use the same license.

Read the official license

Solderpad Hardware License 2.0

Adapted from Apache 2.0 for hardware, with a patent grant and clearer terms for derivative designs.

Read the official license

Creative Commons (docs and images)

Common for tutorials, documentation and images. BY requires attribution, SA requires derivatives under the same license, NC forbids commercial use and ND forbids derivatives.

Read the official license
